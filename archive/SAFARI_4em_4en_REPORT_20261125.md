## 一手逐字核验（Europe PMC 原文摘要）

**§4em**：
> "one's own knowledge consistently contaminates estimates about others' knowledge… When participants were required to answer the question before estimating what novices would know, studying the facts impaired both the calibration and resolution of the estimates… Studying the facts reduced the validity of one's experiences for predicting novices' knowledge… Experimentally reducing reliance on one's own knowledge did not improve the accuracy of estimates… learning impairs the accuracy of judgments of others' knowledge, not because estimators rely too heavily on their own experiences, but because estimators lack diagnostic cues about others' knowledge."

**§4en**：
> "People feel they understand complex phenomena with far greater precision, coherence, and depth than they really do; they are subject to an illusion – an illusion of explanatory depth… The illusion is far stronger for explanatory knowledge than many other kinds of knowledge, such as that for facts, procedures or narratives… The illusion for explanatory knowledge is most robust where the environment supports real-time explanations with visible mechanisms."

## 照见本仓（认知增量）

- **§4em**：①真因不是过度依赖自我而是缺诊断线索——纠偏须提供外部诊断线索而非喊"别主观"（§4dq、§4dz、§4ec）；②「减少依赖自己经验并未提升准确度」——单纯压制自我锚无效（§4eg、§4do）；③校准与分辨力双受损——分别审计"整体准度"与"区分力"（§4ed、§4dz）；④学会后自身经验反失预测效度——专家直觉须重校准（§4ec、§4bv）。
- **§4en**：①自以为懂远超实际——须真能生成解释才算懂（§4ec、§4cu、§4dz）；②解释性知识错觉最强——对因果解释类结论格外审计（§4dj、§4dl）；③可见机制时错觉最稳——"看起来解释得通"≠真懂，须主动生成解释检验（§4ef、§4bv）；④被迫解释可降过度自信——裁定须写出机制而非只给结论（§4cp、§4ch）。
