## 一手逐字核验（Europe PMC 原文摘要）

**§4eg**：
> "Consensus bias is the overuse of self-related knowledge in estimating the prevalence of attributes in a population… The bias seems statistically appropriate (Dawes, 1989), but according to the egocentrism hypothesis, it merely mimics normative inductive reasoning… Even Ss who had been educated about the consensus bias, or had received feedback about actual consensus, or both showed the bias… there was bias even in the presence of unanimous information from 20 randomly chosen others… In all 3 experiments, Ss continued to show consensus bias despite the availability of other statistical information."

**§4eh**：
> "we found a strong misinformation effect overall. In some cases, however, misinformation led to improved recognition, which is opposite the usual finding. Critically, misinformation led to improved recognition of the original event when subjects detected and remembered a change between the original event and the postevent information… detecting and remembering change can enhance retention… The misinformation effect occurs mostly for witnessed details that are not particularly memorable. In the case of more memorable details, providing misinformation can actually facilitate later recollection of the original events."

## 照见本仓（认知增量）

- **§4eg**：①用自己判断估计总体普遍性——须外部基础率校准（§4dq、§4dz、§4ec）；②「教育/反馈/20 人一致信息仍有偏」——披露与告知不足以纠偏，canary/flag 不能只靠"声明就知道"（§4do、§4dx、§4dw）；③「看似合规却只是模仿规范推理」——"走了流程"≠真合规（§4cr、§4cp）；④自我锚与 §4ef 自生成锚调整不足同构。
- **§4eh**：①反证被检测并记住反而提升原事件记忆——暴露于反证可成为校准信号，应保留可检测的变化记录（§4dy、§4ch）；②错误效应主要在低显著细节——须加固"易被改写"的判据（§4bv、§4dp）；③「检测并记住变化才提升」——canary/版本对比须真能被察觉（§4ef、§4cp）；④递归提醒——关键判据反复对照非一次性（§4cx、§4co）。
