## 一手逐字核验（Europe PMC / PubMed 原文摘要）

**§4eq**：
> "Contrary to commonly held beliefs, the available low to moderate quality evidence suggests that both attribute and goal framing may have little if any consistent effect on health consumers' behaviour… The unexplained heterogeneity between studies suggests the possibility of a framing effect under specific conditions." 属性框架理解 SMD -0.58、说服 SMD 0.07、行为 SMD 0.09；目标框架行为 SMD -0.06。

**§4er**：
> "The Allais paradox decision bias was first offered as a challenge to the expected utility theory over 60 years ago… its causes are not well understood… Reducing the magnitude of the outcomes did not affect the size of the Allais paradox, contradicting previous literature and supporting the probability weighting hypothesis… Reducing the probability of the nonzero outcomes to eliminate certainty reduced, but did not eliminate, the Allais paradox, a result inconsistent with probability weighting and other theories… The results suggest that the certainty effect alone cannot explain the Allais paradox."

## 照见本仓（认知增量）

- **§4eq**：①与 §4cn 张力——框架对行为效应近零，不能预设"改表述就一定改结论"（§4ds、§4df）；②认知层有影响（SMD -0.58）但行为层几无——"说得清"≠"照着做"（§4ed、§4do）；③"异质性提示特定条件下有效"——不是无效应而是条件未明，须找边界条件（§4ei、§4cw）；④GRADE 低-中质量证据如实标注。
- **§4er**：①简洁解释（确定性偏好）不足以解释现象——不可用一个"显然机制"覆盖反常，须多机制并存、承认"原因未明"（§4ef、§4en）；②"减少确定性削弱但未消除"——干预部分有效≠机制被证实（§4dh、§4dy）；③概率加权与确定性效应两理论都被部分否定——对立框架都不完整，须找更高层（§4cw、§4ea）；④"60 年未解"——对经典结论保持谦逊（§4ec）。
