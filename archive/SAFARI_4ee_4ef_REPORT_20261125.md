## 一手逐字核验（Europe PMC 原文摘要）

**§4ee**：
> "people reliably associate the rare feature with the Minority population and the frequent feature with the Majority population… This pattern is referred to as 'illusory correlation,' reflecting the standard assumption that such associations are fundamentally irrational. In this article we show that this assumption is incorrect… in fact correct and follows a result in epistemic probability theory known as the 'Rule of Succession'."

**§4ef**：
> "anchor on information that comes to mind and adjust until a plausible estimate is reached… insufficient adjustment is commonly invoked to explain judgmental biases… evidence for adjustment-based anchoring biases has only recently been provided, and the causes of insufficient adjustment remain unclear… adjustments from self-generated anchor values tend to be insufficient because they terminate once a plausible value is reached (Studies 1a and 1b) unless one is able and willing to search for a more accurate estimate (Studies 2a-2c)."

## 照见本仓（认知增量）

- **§4ee**：①"偏差"标签不等于生成结构错误——先检验数字怎么来的，而非贴标签（§4dg 热手非谬误、§4bv）；②主流假设也须证伪检验（§4cw、§4df）；③小样本稀有特征关联在贝叶斯上合理，须算基础率与抽样方差（§4de、§4br）。
- **§4ef**：①"够合理就停手"与"通过名义判据即宣称完成"同构，须主动继续搜索（§4cp、§4co）；②继续搜索是能力+意愿双条件，独立确认集须真执行非名义挂置（§4ch）；③区分"内部生成锚"与"外部给定锚"（§4bv、§4cy）；④对"理所当然的解释"存疑（§4cx、§4cr）。
