## 一、§4dw 社会赞许性量表——检测偏差的工具本身测不到东西

**一手来源**：Lanz L, Thielmann I, Gerpott FH. *Are social desirability scales desirable? A meta-analytic test of the validity of social desirability scales in the context of prosocial behavior*. J Pers. 2022;90(2):203-221. PMID 34265863, DOI 10.1111/jopy.12662。**A 级一手元分析，Europe PMC 逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "These scales are sought to measure individuals' tendencies to present themselves overly positive in self-reports, thus allowing to control for SD biases"
> "research increasingly questions the validity of SD scales, proposing that SD scales measure substantive trait characteristics rather than response bias"
> "we conducted a meta-analysis (k = 41; N = 8980) on the relation between SD scale scores and prosocial behavior in economic games"
> "the meta-analytic correlation between SD scores and prosocial behavior was close to zero, suggesting that SD scales neither clearly measure bias nor substantive traits"
> "scholars and practitioners should refrain from using them"

**照见本仓**：
1. 用来检测偏差的量表本身测不到东西（相关近零）——检测工具/canary/审计规则须先审计其效度，不可假设"设了检测器就能检出偏差"（§4bv、§4dm）；
2. 「neither clearly measure bias nor substantive traits」——结果不支持任何一种预设解释，须接受"工具无效"这一可能而非硬找解释（§4bb、§4cw）；
3. 明确建议停用——发现工具无效应停用而非继续用，对失效判据须有退出机制；
4. k=41、N=8980 大样本+调节分析——结论稳健，接受须多源（§4ch）。

**诚信**：一手摘要逐字核验；外群体同质性检索撞 cookie 墙，如实记录弃用。

## 二、§4dx 坦率假设反转——改了形式，没改实质

**一手来源**：Gnambs T, Kaspar K. *Socially Desirable Responding in Web-Based Questionnaires: A Meta-Analytic Review of the Candor Hypothesis*. Assessment. 2017;24(6):746-762. PMID 26739360, DOI 10.1177/1073191115624547。**A 级一手元分析，PubMed 逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "Unproctored, web-based assessments supposedly reduce social desirability distortions ... because of an increased sense of privacy"
> "Three random-effects meta-analyses ... social desirability (k = 30, total N = 3,746), the Big Five (k = 66, total N = 2,951), or psychopathology (k = 96, total N = 16,034)"
> "Overall, a near-zero effect, Δ = 0.01, was obtained that did not indicate less socially desirable responding in computerized assessments"
> "moderator analyses did not identify differential effects for proctored and unproctored procedures"
> "Unproctored web-based surveys do not offer an advantage"

**照见本仓**：
1. 以为改形式（匿名/线上）就能消除偏差，实测近零（Δ=0.01）——不可假设"换了呈现方式/接口"就减少了偏差，须实测验证（§4ds、§4bv）；
2. 纸笔与计算机均值相当——媒介改变不改变实质回答，须区分形式合规与实质变化；
3. 有监考/无监考无差异——看似更强的隐私保障无效，对"加强版"措施须验证真实增量（§4ds）；
4. 三个元分析 k=30/66/96 大样本——结论稳健，接受须多源（§4ch）。
