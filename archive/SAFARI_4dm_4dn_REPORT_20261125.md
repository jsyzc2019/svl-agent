## 一、§4dm 霍桑效应——被研究本身改变被测行为

**一手来源**：McCambridge J, Witton J, Elbourne DR. *Systematic review of the Hawthorne effect: new concepts are needed to study research participation effects*. J Clin Epidemiol. 2014;67(3):267-77. PMID 24275499, DOI 10.1016/j.jclinepi.2013.08.015。**A 级一手系统综述，Europe PMC 逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "elucidate whether the Hawthorne effect exists ... explore under what conditions ... estimate the size"
> "Nineteen purposively designed studies were included ... eight randomized controlled trials, five quasiexperimental studies, and six observational evaluations"
> "study methods, contexts, and findings were highly heterogeneous"
> "Consequences of research participation for behaviors being investigated do exist, although little can be securely known about the conditions under which they operate, their mechanisms of effects, or their magnitudes. New concepts are needed"

**照见本仓**：
1. 被观察/被研究本身会改变被测行为——"do exist"，测量非中性（§4cd、§4bv）；
2. 「little can be securely known about the conditions/mechanisms/magnitudes」——效应存在但条件/机制/大小都不可靠知道，不得量化依赖，须承认无知（§4ak）；
3. 「highly heterogeneous」+「significant biases judged likely」——异质性大、偏倚风险高，不可把单一观察效应当普适常数（§4de、§4bw）；
4. 「new concepts are needed」——旧框架不足须重建概念，遇反复解释不清的现象应承认框架局限而非硬套（§4cw）。

**诚信**：首次 "Hawthorne effect + meta" 检索错配到一个 ICU 会议增刊（无摘要、无数字），如实弃用，改精确引号检索命中一手系统综述。

## 二、§4dn 问题-行为效应——测到了效应，却不懂机制

**一手来源**：Wood C, Conner M, Miles E, Sandberg T, Taylor N, Godin G, Sheeran P. *The Impact of Asking Intention or Self-Prediction Questions on Subsequent Behavior: A Meta-Analysis*. Pers Soc Psychol Rev. 2016;20(3):245-268. PMID 26162771, DOI 10.1177/1088868315592334。**A 级一手元分析，Europe PMC 逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "Random-effects meta-analysis on 116 published tests ... intention/prediction questions have a small positive effect on behavior (d+ = 0.24)"
> "Little support was observed for attitude accessibility, cognitive dissonance, behavioral simulation, or processing fluency explanations of the QBE"
> "significant effects of social desirability ... difficulty of behavior ... and sample type"
> "future primary research should systematically vary moderators in fully factorial designs"

**照见本仓**：
1. 问意图/自预测问题**本身改变行为**（d+=0.24）——测量工具介入被测量对象，审计/提问/sense 不是中性观察（§4dm、§4bv）；
2. 「Little support for 四种机制」——测到效应却不懂机制，不得把"观察到差异"当成"理解了原因"（§4dj、§4bb）；
3. 效应依社会赞许性/难度/学生样本而变——条件依赖非普适，结论须标注适用条件（§4de、§4cm）；
4. 「应系统变动调节因素做全因子设计」——分离机制须主动变动条件而非被动汇总，须主动构造对照（§4ch）。

**诚信**：四种候选机制"都没被支持"如实照录，不因效应显著就替它脑补一个机制。
