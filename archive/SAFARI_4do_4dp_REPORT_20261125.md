## 一、§4do 利益冲突——披露不等于纠偏

**一手来源**：Lopez J, Lopez S, Means E, et al. *Financial Conflicts of Interest: An Association between Funding and Findings in Plastic Surgery*. Plast Reconstr Surg. 2015;136(5):690e-697e. PMID 26505726, DOI 10.1097/PRS.0000000000001718。**A 级一手，PubMed 原文摘要逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "Of the 1650 abstracts ... 568 fulfilled the inclusion criteria"
> "Financial conflicts of interest were disclosed in only 17.6 percent of the articles"
> "66.2 percent were reviewed as having positive outcomes"
> "The findings reported in each abstract were blindly graded as not applicable, negative, or positive"
> "Studies that disclosed a financial conflict of interest were 7.12 times more likely (p < 0.0001) to present a positive outcome over a negative outcome"

**照见本仓**：
1. 披露了 COI 的研究仍 **7.12 倍**更可能阳性——声明利益关系不改变结论偏向，"声明了立场/披露了方法"不等于消除偏向（§4cp）；
2. 仅 **17.6%** 披露——大量隐藏偏向，未声明的预设/立场比已声明的更危险（§4cr）；
3. 「blindly graded」——盲法评级降低评级者偏向，裁定应隔离"谁做的"与"怎么判"（§4cd、§4cs）；
4. 66.2% 阳性基线——阳性结果本就过度代表，对"通过/阳性"结论保持基线怀疑（§4ct、§4bn）。

**诚信**：一手摘要逐字核验；7.12 倍与盲法评级照录，不因"已披露"就假设偏向消失。

## 二、§4dp 元分析的垃圾进垃圾出——合成不能提升原始质量

**一手来源**：Biondi-Zoccai G, D'Ascenzo F, Frati G, Abbate A. *What meta-analyses teach us: pros and cons*. G Ital Cardiol (Rome). 2015;16(9):561-9. PMID 26418385, DOI 10.1714/1988.21516。**A 级一手，PubMed 原文摘要逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "Meta-analysis ... offers an efficient and potent tool to summarize the clinical evidence"
> "strengths ... statistical precision, external validity, and the opportunity to analyze subgroups and moderators ... also have many limitations"
> "critized because potentially an exercise in 'mega-silliness', mixing 'apples and oranges', unable to improve the quality of primary studies (in keeping with the say 'garbage in-garbage out'), and focusing on an 'average patient' who is only hypothetical"
> "use them critically and constructively, without being dominated nor scared"

**照见本仓**：
1. 「unable to improve the quality of primary studies ... garbage in-garbage out」——合成/汇总不能提升原始证据质量，合并审计/多数表决不会自动提高底层判据可靠性（§4ch、§4ck）；
2. 「mixing apples and oranges」——异质研究被混合，须先分层再汇总（§4de、§4cm）；
3. 「average patient who is only hypothetical」——平均对象不存在，"平均指标/总体结论"可能不对应任何真实个案（§4de、§4bv）；
4. 优点真实但须批判使用——对汇总既不全盘否定也不盲信（§4cw）；
5. 「without being dominated」——对工具保持主体性，裁定权归用户不被任何汇总结果支配。

**诚信**：一手摘要逐字核验；优点与批评并列照录，不因"元分析是高级证据"就免于批判。
