## 一、§4ea 心理距离效应——跨时间/研究者/情境才可信

**一手来源**：Soderberg CK, Callahan SP, Kochersberger AO, Amit E, Ledgerwood A. *The effects of psychological distance on abstraction: Two meta-analyses*. Psychol Bull. 2015;141(3):525-548. PMID 25420220, DOI 10.1037/bul0000005。**A 级一手元分析，Europe PMC 逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "Across 106 papers containing a total of 267 experiments, our results showed a reliable and medium-sized effect of psychological distance on both level of abstraction in mental representation and the downstream consequences of abstraction"
> "Importantly, these effects replicate across time, researchers, and settings"
> "several key moderators, including the size of the difference in distance between 2 levels of a temporal distance manipulation and the dependent variable's capacity to tap processing of both abstract and concrete features"

**照见本仓**：106 篇 267 实验跨时间/研究者/情境稳健复制——效应可信须多维复制（§4ch、§4co）；明确"中等效应"而非只说"存在"，结论须量化幅度（§4bv）；两个调节因素——效应有条件，须标注边界（§4de、§4cm）。与 §4eb 形成张力：同领域既有稳健效应也有崩塌效应，不可整体接受或整体怀疑，须逐个结论现场检验（§4bb、§4cw）。

## 二、§4eb 高引用发现复制失败——样本越大效应越小

**一手来源**：Calderon S, Mac Giolla E, Ask K, Granhag PA. *Subjective likelihood and the construal level of future events: A replication study of Wakslak, Trope, Liberman, and Alony (2006)*. J Pers Soc Psychol. 2020;119(5):e27-e37. PMID 32673044, DOI 10.1037/pspa0000214。**A 级一手，Europe PMC 逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "This well-cited, but understudied finding has had a major influence"
> "2 close replication attempts (N = 115 and N = 120; the original studies had N = 20 and N = 34) that failed to find the effect"
> "Bayesian analyses provided diagnostic support for the absence of an effect"
> "a strong trend of declining effect sizes as a function of larger samples"
> "advise against treating likelihood as a psychological distance until further tests"

**照见本仓**：原始小样本（N=20/34）效应大、复制大样本（N=115/120）效应消失且"样本越大效应越小"——赢家诅咒，小样本初验须默认降权待复核（§4co、§4ct）；「well-cited but understudied」——引用数不等于可靠性（§4dj、§4cf）；贝叶斯支持"无效应"——可正面确认"无效应"而非只说"没显著"，须区分"未证实"与"已证伪"（§4bb）；作者主动建议撤回——本仓须有结论撤回/降权机制。
