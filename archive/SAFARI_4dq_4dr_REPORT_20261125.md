## 一、§4dq 优于平均——慢性高估，临时提醒纠不了

**一手来源**：Varnum ME. *Higher in status, (Even) better-than-average*. Front Psychol. 2015;6:496. PMID 25972824, DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00496。**A 级一手，Europe PMC 逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "In 5 studies (total N = 1357) ... the relationship between socioeconomic status (SES) and the better-than-average effect (BTAE) was tested"
> "subjective measures of SES were positively correlated with magnitude of BTAE. Effects of objective measures (income and education) were weaker and less consistent"
> "manipulations of SES ... did not have strong effects on BTAE magnitude (d's ranging from -0.04 to -0.14)"
> "chronic, but not temporary, status may be linked with a stronger tendency to overestimate one's abilities and positive traits"

**照见本仓**：
1. 人系统性高估自己优于平均——裁定者对自身判据的置信须默认怀疑（§4cf、§4cu、§4db）；
2. 高估幅度与**主观**地位正相关而客观指标更弱——高估依附"自我感觉"而非客观事实，须区分自我评估与外部证据（§4bv）；
3. 长期地位显著但**临时操纵无效**（d=-0.04~-0.14）——偏差是慢性稳定倾向，临时提醒/一次性校准纠不了，须制度性持续约束（§4cp）；
4. 结论经元分析稳健，接受须多样本（§4ch）。

**诚信**：一手摘要逐字核验；参考类预测检索零结果如实弃用，不硬凑。

## 二、§4dr 自我提升——动机普适，但比较维度须先校准

**一手来源**：Sedikides C, Gaertner L, Vevea JL. *Pancultural self-enhancement reloaded: a meta-analytic reply to Heine (2005)*. J Pers Soc Psychol. 2005;89(4):539-51. PMID 16287417, DOI 10.1037/0022-3514.89.4.539。**A 级一手元分析，PubMed 逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "the authors carried out 2 meta-analytic investigations. The results backed the ... theory and findings"
> "Both Westerners and Easterners self-enhanced tactically"
> "Westerners self-enhanced on attributes relevant to the cultural ideal of individualism, whereas Easterners self-enhanced on attributes relevant to the cultural ideal of collectivism (in both cases, because of the personal importance of the ideal)"
> "Self-enhancement motivation is universal, although its manifestations are strategically sensitive to cultural context"
> "why researchers should empirically validate the comparison dimension"

**照见本仓**：
1. 自我提升动机普适但体现在哪个维度取决于价值框架——同一"偏差"在不同框架下表现不同，不可跨框架直接比较幅度（§4cn、§4dq）；
2. 「empirically validate the comparison dimension」——先校准比较维度才能谈差异，须先验证判据维度本身有效再比较结果（§4bv、§4dp）；
3. 「because of the personal importance of the ideal」——偏差落在被重视的维度，裁定者在"最在意的判据"上最容易自欺（§4cu、§4db）；
4. 以证据回应质疑——本仓须以证据而非权威支撑结论（§4bb）。

**诚信**：一手摘要逐字核验；这是元分析对批评的回应，结论以证据辩护而非权威宣称。
