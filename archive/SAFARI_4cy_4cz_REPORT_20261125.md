## §4cy · 锚定效应（A 级一手元分析）
一手：Bystranowski, Janik, Próchnicki & Skórska, *Anchoring effect in legal decision-making: A meta-analysis*, Law Hum Behav 2021;45(1):1-23，PMID 33734746。逐字："numeric decision-making in law is susceptible to the effect of (possibly arbitrary) values present in the decision contexts"；"Twenty-nine studies (93 effect sizes; N = 8,549)"；有对照组 d=0.58 [95%CI .44,.73]、无对照组 d=0.91 [.69,1.12] 均显著，"although we provide some evidence of possible publication bias"；"legal expertise or the anchor relevance" 为调节因素。

照见本仓：判据环境中的任意数值实质选择结论（§4bv/§4cc）；N=8549 效应稳健；作者自陈可能发表偏倚、照录不隐（§4bn）；法律专业性也未必免疫锚定（§4cu）。

**诚信**：anchoring 首轮 "Boochler" 拼写不在 PubMed、零结果如实记录，简化后命中。

## §4cz · 确认偏误的独立性近似根因（A 级一手）
一手：Pilgrim, Sanborn, Malthouse & Hills, *Confirmation bias emerges from an approximation to Bayesian reasoning*, Cognition 2024;245:105693，PMID 38244398。逐字："confirmation bias emerges as a natural consequence of boundedly rational belief updating by presenting the BIASR model (Bayesian updating with an Independence Approximation and Source Reliability)"；"the tracking of which represents an unrealistic demand on memory"；"assuming independence between beliefs"；生成 "biased evaluation, biased assimilation, attitude polarisation, belief perseverance and confirmation bias in the selection of sources"。

照见本仓：确认偏误是有界理性的自然结果、非道德缺陷——靠结构非提醒（§4cp）；"假设信念独立"是把不独立证据当独立、是 §4ck 有效独立票数的机制级根因；追踪依赖不可能须外部验证者（§4ch）；五类偏误多形态、单一防护不够；信源可信度须显式更新（§4cd）。

**诚信**：confirmation 首轮把 Nickerson 当作者名检索出一堆同名无关论文，检索错配如实记录未采，简化后命中。
