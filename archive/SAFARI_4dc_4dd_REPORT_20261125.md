## §4dc · 人际沉没成本（A 级一手）
一手：Olivola CY, *The Interpersonal Sunk-Cost Effect*, Psychol Sci 2018;29(7):1072-1083，PMID 29750633。逐字："pursuing an inferior alternative merely because we have previously invested significant, but nonrecoverable, resources"；"it is also an interpersonal effect (i.e., people will alter their choices in response to other people's past investments)"；"Across eight experiments (N = 6,076) ... sunk-cost effects when the costs are borne by someone other than the decision maker"；"not moderated by social closeness or whether other people observe their sunk costs being 'honored'"。

照见本仓：别人的/前人的投入也绑架决策，历史轮次不作继续理由（§4cl）；N=6076 稳健；无社会距离调节；nonrecoverable 只看未来不看已投入（§4cj）。
