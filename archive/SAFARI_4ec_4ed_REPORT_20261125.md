## 一、§4ec 直觉专长的边界——看环境结构，不看自信感

**一手来源**：Kahneman D, Klein G. *Conditions for intuitive expertise: a failure to disagree*. Am Psychol. 2009;64(6):515-526. PMID 19739881, DOI 10.1037/a0016755。**A 级一手，PubMed 原文摘要逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "professional intuition is sometimes marvelous and sometimes flawed"
> "map the boundary conditions that separate true intuitive skill from overconfident and biased impressions"
> "evaluating the likely quality of an intuitive judgment requires an assessment of the predictability of the environment in which the judgment is made and of the individual's opportunity to learn the regularities of that environment"
> "Subjective experience is not a reliable indicator of judgment accuracy"

**照见本仓**：
1. 直觉有时极佳有时糟糕，分界在**环境可预测性+学习机会**——判断判据可靠吗须先看环境是否规律稳定、裁定者是否有学习机会（§4cf、§4cu、§4bv）；
2. 「Subjective experience is not a reliable indicator of judgment accuracy」——自信感不能替代证据，须外部基础率/反馈校准（§4dq、§4dz、§4dl）；
3. 两大对立流派（启发式偏差 vs 自然决策）"failure to disagree"收敛于共同边界——面对对立解释应找更高层条件而非选边（§4cw、§4cz）。

## 二、§4ed 会算均值，不等于会算不确定性

**一手来源**：Herdener N, Wickens CD, Clegg BA, Smith CAP. *Overconfidence in Projecting Uncertain Spatial Trajectories*. Hum Factors. 2016;58(6):899-914. PMID 27125532, DOI 10.1177/0018720816645259。**A 级一手，PubMed 原文摘要逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "participants greatly underestimated the variance in predicted location, indicating overconfidence"
> "the effect was replicated with frequencies rather than probabilities"
> "Overconfidence was reduced on the more difficult predictions"
> "those better at predicting the mean were not better at predicting the variance"
> "qualitatively different abilities than prediction of the mean"
> "Improving real-world performance at prediction demands developing better understanding of variability, not just the average case"

**照见本仓**：
1. 预测均值准但严重低估方差——会算中心不代表会算不确定性，不可因"指标算对了"宣称"风险已估准"（§4cf、§4bv）；
2. 「those better at predicting the mean were not better at predicting the variance」——两种能力质不同，须分别审计均值判断与置信区间判断（§4br、§4dl）；
3. 频率代概率仍复制——换表述不消除偏差（§4dx、§4cn）；
4. 「Overconfidence was reduced on the more difficult predictions」——困难触发谨慎，校准随难度变化，须分层评估置信（§4cf、§4de）；
5. 须理解变异性而非只看平均——结论须附不确定性（§4cq、§4dp）。
