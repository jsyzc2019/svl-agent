## 一手逐字核验（Europe PMC / PubMed 原文摘要）

**§4es**：
> "a meta-analysis of ego-depletion experiments found a medium-sized effect, subsequent meta-analyses have questioned the size and existence of the effect and identified instances of possible bias… Multiple laboratories (k = 23, total N = 2,141) conducted replications of a standardized ego-depletion protocol… the size of the ego-depletion effect was small with 95% confidence intervals (CIs) that encompassed zero (d = 0.04, 95% CI [-0.07, 0.15])."

**§4et**：
> "We estimated parameters from 268 curve estimates drawn from 81 articles and revealed that the mere exposure effect was characterized by a positive slope and negative quadratic effect consistent with an inverted-U shaped curve… such curves were associated with (a) all visual, but not auditory stimuli; (b) exposure durations shorter than 10 s and longer than 1 min… (d) ratings that were taken after all stimuli were presented… existing models for the mere exposure effect do not adequately account for the findings."

## 照见本仓（认知增量）

- **§4es**：①经典中等效应在 23 实验室预注册标准化复制下缩到 d=0.04 且 CI 跨零——对实测效应保持可复制性谦逊（§4co、§4eb）；②元分析结论也可被推翻，不把"一次 meta"当终局（§4dp、§4ct）；③RRR 预注册范式是预注册锁/flag 分层的科学依据（§4cp、§4cr）；④标准化下仍近零——找边界条件而非断言有或无（§4eq、§4ei）。
- **§4et**：①"重复就喜欢"实为倒 U——对"越多越好/越查越稳"的线性假设警惕，可能有最优点（§4dt、§4dh）；②强边界条件——结论须附适用条件（§4ei、§4eq）；③现有模型不足——承认框架不全（§4er、§4en）；④区分 liking/familiarity/recognition 三层——分层看"熟悉/认可/偏好"（§4ed、§4cs）。
