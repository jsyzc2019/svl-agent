## 一、§4ds 默认效应被高估——真正起作用的往往是隐藏机制

**一手来源**：Molina-Pérez A, Rodríguez-Arias D, Delgado J. *Differential impact of opt-in, opt-out policies on deceased organ donation rates: a mixed conceptual and empirical study*. BMJ Open. 2022;12(10):e057107. PMID 36441113, DOI 10.1136/bmjopen-2021-057107。**A 级一手，PubMed 原文摘要逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "Although opt-out countries tend to have higher donation rates, there is no conclusive evidence that this is caused by the policy itself"
> "opt-in and opt-out have strictly identical outcomes in eight out of nine situations. They only differ when neither the deceased nor the family have expressed a preference and defaults therefore apply"
> "The direct impact of consent policy defaults is typically circumscribed to a range of 0%-5% of all opportunities for organ recovery"
> "the intervention of the family improves organ retrieval under opt-in but hinders it under opt-out"
> "might be overestimating the influence of the default and underestimating the power granted to families"

**照见本仓**：
1. 默认/选择架构的直接影响常被夸大（仅 0%-5%、9 情境 8 个相同）——不可把"改了默认/改了呈现"当决定性杠杆，须量化真实边际（§4cn、§4bv）；
2. 差异只在**没人表态**时才出现——默认只在信息真空处起作用，须识别哪些判据处于真空 vs 已有明确立场（§4cp）；
3. 家庭介入在 opt-in 改善、opt-out 阻碍——同一机制在不同制度下方向反转，警惕"同一措施普适有益"（§4cn、§4de）；
4. 「no conclusive evidence caused by policy itself」——国别相关被误当因果（§4dj、§4cm）。

**诚信**：一手摘要逐字核验；choice overload 初次检索错配到决策支持工具研究，如实弃用。

## 二、§4dt 选择过载——选项过多有害，但人也夸张这个效应

**一手来源**：Hu X, Meng Z, He Q. *Choice overload interferes with early processing and necessitates late compensation: Evidence from electroencephalogram*. Eur J Neurosci. 2024;59(11):2995-3008. PMID 38575329, DOI 10.1111/ejn.16322。**A 级一手，Europe PMC 逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "an increase in the number of options led to negative evaluations and avoidance of choice tendencies, even in the absence of time pressure"
> "the large choice set interfered with the early visual process, as evidenced by the small P1 amplitude, and failed to attract more attentional resources in the early stage, as evidenced by the small amplitude of P2 and N2"
> "the LPC amplitude was increased in the late stage, suggesting greater investment of attentional resources and higher emotional arousal"
> "the difference ... began at around 120 ms, and the early and late stages were characterised by opposite activation patterns"
> "individuals tend to subjectively exaggerate the choice overload effect"

**照见本仓**：
1. 选项增多导致负评价+回避倾向（即使无时间压力）——判据/选项过多不是更严谨而是超负荷，须主动简化判据集（§4cq）；
2. 大选项集干扰早期加工（P1/P2/N2 小）+后期 LPC 补偿——超载先损害初步筛选再靠后期耗费补偿（§4dm、§4cf）；
3. 「early and late stages ... opposite activation patterns」——表面后期补偿掩盖了早期损害，不可只看最终结果须看过程（§4bv、§4dm）；
4. 「individuals subjectively exaggerate the choice overload effect」——人对效应本身也夸张，两端都要校准：既不信"越多越好"也不夸"越多越糟"（§4cf、§4dq）。

**诚信**：一手摘要逐字核验；明确外部神经科学仅作哲学镜像，不宣称本仓有同样神经机制。
