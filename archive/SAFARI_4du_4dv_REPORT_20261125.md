## 一、§4du 禀赋效应反转——表面差异不等于谁有偏

**一手来源**：Yechiam E, Ashby NJS, Pachur T. *Who's biased? A meta-analysis of buyer-seller differences in the pricing of lotteries*. Psychol Bull. 2017;143(5):543-563. PMID 28263644, DOI 10.1037/bul0000095。**A 级一手元分析，Europe PMC 逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "the typical finding being that selling prices exceed buying prices (i.e., the endowment effect)"
> "The meta-analysis, which includes 35 articles, indicates that selling prices considerably exceed buying prices (Cohen's d = 0.58)"
> "selling prices deviate less from the lotteries' expected values than buying prices, both in absolute and in relative terms"
> "buyers have lower probability sensitivity and a more pronounced response bias"
> "challenges the prominent account whereby sellers' valuations are upward biased due to loss aversion, and supports alternative theoretical accounts"

**照见本仓**：
1. 卖价高于买价（d=0.58）但卖价反而**更接近规范值**——表面差异不等于谁有偏，须用外部规范基准判定偏向方向而非凭"谁偏离常识"（§4bv、§4dj）；
2. 标准解释（卖家因损失厌恶上偏）被挑战——流行归因未必对，对"显然的解释"保持怀疑并找替代理论（§4bb、§4cw）；
3. 真正有偏的是**买方**（概率敏感度低+反应偏向强）——须检查是哪一方在误用判据（§4dl）；
4. 35 篇元分析+多种偏向指标交叉验证——单一指标不足定论（§4bw）。

**诚信**：一手摘要逐字核验；损失厌恶专用检索零结果如实弃用，不硬凑。

## 二、§4dv 自我-他人风险决策——平均无差异掩盖反向调节

**一手来源**：Batteux E, Ferguson E, Tunney RJ. *Do our risk preferences change when we make decisions for others? A meta-analysis of self-other differences in decisions involving risk*. PLoS One. 2019;14(5):e0216566. PMID 31067283, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0216566。**A 级一手元分析，Europe PMC 逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "We reviewed 78 effect sizes from 49 studies (7,576 participants)"
> "There was no overall self-other difference, but there were moderating effects of domain and frame"
> "Decisions in the interpersonal domain were more risk-averse for self than for other. Decisions in the medical domain were more risk-seeking for self than for other"
> "no overall self-other differences in the financial domain, however there was a moderating effect of frame: decisions in a gain frame were more risk-averse for self than other whereas decisions in a loss frame were more risk-seeking for self than other"

**照见本仓**：
1. 「no overall difference」但有调节——合并平均零不代表无差异，不可因总体指标无差异就宣布"无问题"（§4de、§4dp）；
2. 人际域自更规避/医疗域自更冒险——同一"自我偏差"在不同领域**方向相反**，须分领域裁定不可统一结论（§4de、§4cm）；
3. 收益框架自更规避/损失框架自更冒险——框架决定方向，须显式标注判据所处框架（§4cn）；
4. 49 研究 7576 人——多样本稳健，接受须多源（§4ch）；作者自陈须看情境，结论须标注适用边界（§4cm）。

**诚信**：一手摘要逐字核验；平均无差异与反向调节并列照录，不因"总体无差异"就宣布无问题。
