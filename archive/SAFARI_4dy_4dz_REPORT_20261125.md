## 一、§4dy 辟谣是双刃剑——纠正不等于清除

**一手来源**：Chan MS, Jones CR, Hall Jamieson K, Albarracín D. *Debunking: A Meta-Analysis of the Psychological Efficacy of Messages Countering Misinformation*. Psychol Sci. 2017;28(11):1531-1546. PMID 28895452, DOI 10.1177/0956797617714579。**A 级一手元分析，Europe PMC 逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "This meta-analysis (k = 52, N = 6,878) revealed large effects for presenting misinformation (ds = 2.41-3.08), debunking (ds = 1.14-1.33), and the persistence of misinformation in the face of debunking (ds = 0.75-1.06)"
> "Persistence was stronger and the debunking effect was weaker when audiences generated reasons in support of the initial misinformation"
> "A detailed debunking message correlated positively with the debunking effect. Surprisingly, however, a detailed debunking message also correlated positively with the misinformation-persistence effect"

**照见本仓**：
1. 辟谣有效（d=1.14-1.33）但错误信息仍持续（d=0.75-1.06）——纠正不等于清除，一次澄清后须持续验证旧结论是否仍在起作用（§4cr、§4cx）；
2. 受众自己生成支持理由时持续更强、辟谣更弱——让相关方自证会加固错误，须警惕"让提出者自己解释理由"反而加固立场（§4cz）；
3. 详细辟谣同时增强辟谣与持续——"给更多信息"是把双刃剑，不可假设补充细节就是纠偏，须实测净效应（§4dx、§4ds）；
4. 52 研究 6878 人——稳健，接受须多源（§4ch）。

**诚信**：一手摘要逐字核验；元认知初次检索撞 cookie 墙，如实记录转精确检索。

## 二、§4dz 延迟判断——即时自信不可靠

**一手来源**：Rhodes MG, Tauber SK. *The influence of delaying judgments of learning on metacognitive accuracy: a meta-analytic review*. Psychol Bull. 2011;137(1):131-148. PMID 21219059, DOI 10.1037/a0021705。**A 级一手元分析，Europe PMC 逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "delaying predictions serves to substantially increase the relative accuracy of JOLs compared with soliciting JOLs immediately after study, a finding termed the delayed JOL effect"
> "4,554 participants (112 effect sizes) ... delaying JOLs leads to robust benefits to relative accuracy (g = 0.93)"
> "3,807 participants (98 effect sizes) ... delayed JOLs result in a modest but reliable benefit for memory performance ... (g = 0.08)"
> "delayed JOL accuracy reflects access to more diagnostic information from long-term memory rather than being a by-product of a retrieval opportunity"

**照见本仓**：
1. 即时判断远不如延迟判断准（g=0.93）——即时"看起来对"的置信不可靠，须延迟/冷静后再裁定（§4cf、§4cx）；
2. 延迟更准是因接触**更多诊断性信息**——准确判断依赖信息基础而非"多看几遍"，须检查判断时是否有充分诊断信息（§4bv）；
3. 相对准确性 g=0.93 但记忆表现仅 g=0.08——判断更准不等于结果更好，须区分"评估准"与"做得好"（§4br）；
4. 大样本稳健（§4ch）；作者自陈多种机制可能并存，不硬套单一解释（§4cw）。

**诚信**：一手摘要逐字核验；g=0.93（评估准）与 g=0.08（表现好）的差别照录，不混为一谈。
