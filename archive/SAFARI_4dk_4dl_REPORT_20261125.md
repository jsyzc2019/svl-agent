## 一、§4dk 合取谬误——细节越多，概率反而越低

**一手来源**：Ciosk MA, Hegarty P. *Unthinkable gay Black men: Asymmetric conjunction fallacies at the intersection of race and sexual orientation*. Br J Soc Psychol. 2026;65(1):e70032. PMID 41392403。**A 级一手，PubMed 原文摘要逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "Five studies with 1226 participants evidence that these stereotypes produce classic conjunction fallacy violations of extensional reasoning"
> "a man with Black-stereotypic attributes was deemed more likely to be both Black and gay than to be gay"
> "These effects were not equivalent"
> "'Black' may be dominant over 'gay' in impression formation at this intersection"

**照见本仓**：
1. 合取概率 P(A∩B) **不可能超过** P(A) 或 P(B)，但刻板印象让人违反外延推理——细节越多"看起来越可能"实则概率更低，警惕"多个判据同时满足看起来更可信"实则合取概率更低（§4cq）；
2. 效应**不对称**（Black 主导 gay）——哪个标签主导决定判断方向，警惕主导框架/最先标签决定裁定（§4cn）；
3. 新信息**抑制判断而非中性更新**——检查新证据是否真的改变判断还是被同化（§4cz、§4cx）；
4. 5 研究 1226 人——效应稳健非单例，接受结论须多样本验证（§4ch）。

**诚信**：一手摘要逐字核验；刻板印象内容仅作哲学镜像，不外推本仓个案判断。

## 二、§4dl 贝叶斯更新欠用——给了证据也不被充分采纳

**一手来源**：Rottman BM. *Physician Bayesian updating from personal beliefs about the base rate and likelihood ratio*. Mem Cognit. 2017;45(2):270-280. PMID 27752962, DOI 10.3758/s13421-016-0658-z。**A 级一手方法学，PubMed 撞 cookie 墙转 Europe PMC 逐字核验**。

**逐字要点**：
> "rarely have subjects been allowed to use their own preexisting beliefs about the prior and the likelihood"
> "the posttest judgments were strongly related to the physicians' beliefs about both the prior probability as well as the likelihood ratio, and the priors were used considerably more strongly than in previous research"
> "both the prior and the likelihoods were still not used quite as much as they should have been, and there was evidence of other nonnormative aspects to the updating, such as updating independent of the likelihood beliefs"
> "there is reason to be optimistic about experts' abilities, but that there is still considerable need for improvement"

**照见本仓**：
1. 专家被允许用自己的先验/似然时更新更接近规范但仍**欠用**——不能假设"给了证据就会被充分采纳"（§4cd）；
2. 「updating independent of the likelihood beliefs」——存在完全不看证据强度就更新判断的非规范成分，须分离"看到了证据"与"证据真的驱动了结论"（§4bv）；
3. 先验使用比以往强——先验权重可调，须显式声明先验/默认判定而非隐含（§4bl）；
4. 「乐观但需改进」——专家非无能也非完美，对自身裁定保持校准的谦逊（§4cf、§4cu）。

**诚信**：PubMed 页面撞 cookie 墙，如实记录为访问受阻，改 Europe PMC 一手库逐字核验，不编造。
