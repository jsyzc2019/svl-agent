A transferable rationale can require a different
implementation. The coding runs inherit the
same completion gate, but the SIM run extends it
to check target-specific requirements (§F.3). The
need for completion evidence carries over; what
counts as sufficient evidence changes. This illus-
trates how earlier reasoning can guide adapta-
tion without treating the source implementation
as fixed.


New outcomes add evidence to an existing di-
agnosis. P3 links a source diagnosis to fur-
ther target interventions and their outcomes
(§F.4). Keeping these attempts together lets later
rounds inspect which remedies were tried, un-
der which settings, and which predictions re-
main unresolved. The reusable experience in-
cludes the evidence behind a diagnosis, not only
its name.


Experience remains available after an edit is
replaced. P8 retains the selected blocking at-
tempt and its unresolved predictions after later
edits change the behavior (§F.2). The final har-
ness alone would omit this part of the search
history. SIM keeps the attempt, its diagnosis,
and its unresolved outcome together as the har-
ness changes.
