三、金矿：被中文报道弱化的那半句

中文转载普遍聚焦"数字曲线 + 行业红线 + 2027 预警"，却几乎都弱化了原文这句：
"we should also expect further degradation in currently dominant forms of alignment auditing and further increases in evaluation and training awareness. I believe frontier model developers should jointly endeavour to establish shared norms for how to evaluate such models and red-team safety cases for them by the time they are first used, including shared transparency standards."
这半句对本仓的意义：它从最强的第三方权威口吻确认了本仓一直在守的那条纪律——被审对象会越来越懂评测，所以审计判据必须放在它够不到的地方。本仓从 §4S25 修 verify_external 跳过缺失样本的洞 → §4S32 验证器的验证器 → §4S37/§4S38 Claude Code "keep its verifiers honest" → §4S40 反一味拒答与排列不变性，走的正是"审计退化"的反方向。这不是我自己的 paranoid，是有一手背书的。
